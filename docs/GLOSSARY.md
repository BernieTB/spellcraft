# Glossary

Key terms of Spellcraft. Mechanics are described in [`GAME_DESIGN.md`](GAME_DESIGN.md); this file only defines
words. Use these terms consistently in code, docs and issues.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Bestiary** | Permanent record of knowledge about enemies, kept across runs. Holds the professor information revealed by mini-bosses (cards, health, shield), shown during the preparation phase. The length of the professor's spell line is always shown; a revealed card is recorded by its position in the line (so two copies of a card are told apart) with its card id, and if the professor's data changed since, that card counts as unknown again (both confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-04). Saved locally as versioned JSON after **every reveal** (owner decision of 2026-10-04). In code: `Game.Core.Meta.Bestiary` (knowledge), `Game.Unity.Meta.FileBestiaryStore` (save file). See [Meta-progression](GAME_DESIGN.md#meta-progression) and [ADR 0014](adr/0014-boss-preparation-and-recap.md). |
| **Biome** | One section of a run, representing one year at the magic school. Contains regular monsters, secret rooms and a professor. See [Run structure](GAME_DESIGN.md#run-structure). In code: `Game.Core.Runs.BiomeDefinition` (authored as `Game.Unity.Runs.BiomeAsset`); the Vertical slice has a placeholder one, `BIOME_01`. |
| **Card** | One element of the spell line. Each card is a word. Cards react to their neighbours and evolve through use. See [Spell line](GAME_DESIGN.md#spell-line). |
| **Card instance** | One copy of a card owned during a run. Two copies of the same card are two instances, so each tracks its own state (such as evolution). In code: `Game.Core.Runs.CardInstance`. |
| **Card summary** | What a card shows on any screen: cast time, its effects with the amounts of the stage it is at, the neighbour bonuses it gives and its stage mark, in compact lines on every card slot (run screen line and reserve, preparation, recap, level-up) with the full details in a hover detail panel (`HoverDetail`; the native tooltip is editor-only). Passive upgrades the hero owns are listed the same way on the run screen. A pure view-model, no rule. In code: `Game.Unity.UI.Cards.CardSummary`, `PassiveSummary`. |
| **Cast time** | The number of ticks a card takes to cast. Its effects resolve when the cast ends. See [ADR 0002](adr/0002-first-pass-combat-rules.md). |
| **Class** | A playable kind of character: the hero's base stats, starting spell line and the card pool of its level-up offers. In code: `Game.Unity.Classes.ClassAsset` (data), `Game.Core.Classes.ClassDefinition` (Core). The MVP class is the combo weaver, working name `CLASS_A`, with placeholder cards `CARD_A` to `CARD_N` ([ADR 0007](adr/0007-mvp-class-and-starting-deck.md)). |
| **Combat log** | The ordered list of events of a fight (card cast, damage, healing, shield gain, death, line change, evolution), each tied to its tick, card, position in the line, caster and target. A card cast also shows the neighbour bonus it received and the part of it that was wasted. Read by tests, the debug fight viewer and, later, the recap. In code: `Game.Core.Combat.Log.CombatLog`. |
| **Combatant** | A participant in a fight: the player character or an enemy. Holds the combat stats: health and shield. Starting values come from data. In code: `Game.Core.Combat.Combatant`. |
| **Dead** | State of a combatant whose current health has reached zero. A dead combatant takes no damage, cannot be healed and gains no shield (placeholder rule: no revival mechanic is designed). |
| **Encounter** | The enemies the hero faces together in one fight, in resolution order (the hero aims at the first living one). Authored as data; the same enemy can appear several times. In code: `Game.Unity.Enemies.EncounterAsset` (data), `Game.Core.Enemies.EncounterDefinition` (Core). |
| **Enemy** | Any opponent of the hero: a regular monster, a mini-boss or a professor (its **rank**). Has its own stats and spell line, from data, and fights with the same rules as the hero. In code: `Game.Unity.Enemies.EnemyAsset` (data), `Game.Core.Enemies.EnemyDefinition` (Core). |
| **Fight session** | One fight of a run played tick by tick (`Run.BeginFight`), so the player can edit the spell line during a regular fight. Its changes update the run's card instances at once; completing it counts the fight (XP, levels, outcome) like `Run.Play`, which is a session with no change. A fight left unfinished is cancelled: it is not counted, but the line changes already made stay. While a session is open, nothing else can change the run, except taking a pending level-up (its passive upgrade applies from the next fight). Both rules confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-04. In code: `Game.Core.Runs.RunFightSession`. |
| **Fight time limit** | The single global maximum length of a fight in ticks, from data (a safety net far above a normal fight): a fight that reaches it is a defeat of the hero ([ADR 0011](adr/0011-enemy-tiers-and-fight-time-limit.md)). In code: `Game.Unity.Runs.FightTimeLimitAsset`, read as `RunRules.FightTimeLimit`. |
| **Evolution** | A card instance growing stronger the more it is cast during a run: two **stages**, reached after a number of casts set in its data. Its word and cast time never change. Each copy of a card counts its own casts over the whole run, in the line or the reserve. A stage takes effect on the next cast of the copy (the cast that reached it resolved with the old stage; confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-04). In code: `Game.Core.Cards.CardEvolution` (a stage), `CardDefinition.AtStage`, `Game.Core.Runs.CardInstance.Casts`. Shown on the run screen by a stage mark (`*` or `**`, `CardSlotViewModel.StageMarkText`) and a short highlight when it evolves; there is no progress gauge. See [ADR 0013](adr/0013-card-evolution.md). |
| **Game flow** | The state machine that chains the screens of the game: title, run, level-up choice, preparation, recap, end of run, back to the title. It holds no rule (Core and the run screen controller decide what is possible) and owns the bestiary between runs, saving it after every reveal. The seed of each run comes from the clock, outside Core. In code: `Game.Unity.Flow.GameFlow` (plain C#), shown by `GameFlowPresenter`, started by `GameFlowBehaviour` in the bootstrap scene. |
| **Grimoire** | Meta-progression collection of discovered named spells. |
| **Health** | Combat stat. **Max health** is the upper bound, set by data; **current health** is what remains, between zero and max health. Damage not absorbed by shield lowers current health (never below zero); healing raises it (never above max health). |
| **Level** | The hero's level in a run, starting at 1, reached through XP along the level curve. In code: `Run.Level`. See [ADR 0009](adr/0009-vertical-slice-run-pacing.md). |
| **Level curve** | The XP cost of each level-up, from data: an explicit cost for each early level-up, each greater than the previous, then each next level costs a fixed amount more, with no level cap. In code: `Game.Core.Runs.LevelCurve` (authored as `Game.Unity.Runs.LevelCurveAsset`), part of `RunRules`. |
| **Level-up** | Reaching the next level. Several can happen in one fight. Each one waits as a **pending level-up** until the player takes its linked choice. In code: `Run.PendingLevelUps`, `Run.ConsumePendingLevelUp`, `RunFightReport.LevelsGained`. |
| **Level-up screen** | The UI Toolkit screen where the player takes the linked choice of a pending level-up: it shows each offered package (the card and the passive upgrade), the new card goes to the end of the line, and when the line is full the player picks the reserve (default) or a line card to replace. The position of the new card is not chosen (the line can be rearranged afterwards). See [ADR 0012](adr/0012-linked-choices-and-spell-line-editing.md). In code: `Game.Unity.UI.LevelUpScreenModel` (view-model), `LevelUpScreenView`; the choice is applied by `Run.TakeLevelUpPackage`. |
| **Line change** | A change the player makes to the hero's spell line during a regular fight: move a card within the line, or swap a line card with a reserve card. It applies at the start of its tick: the card being cast finishes its cast, and pending neighbour bonuses stay at their position. A card moved while cast resolves at its new position and the line continues after it; a card swapped out while cast still resolves, at the position it left, with the bonus it started with; several changes on one tick apply in order (confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-04). A cast takes its pending bonus when it starts. Mini-boss and professor fights refuse it. Recorded in the combat log. In code: `Game.Core.Combat.LineChange`. See [ADR 0012](adr/0012-linked-choices-and-spell-line-editing.md) and [ADR 0015](adr/0015-live-spell-line-editing-details.md). |
| **Linked choice** | The level-up reward: a package of one passive upgrade plus one card, among 3 offered. The player picks a package, not the two parts separately. See [Character and level-ups](GAME_DESIGN.md#character-and-level-ups). The offer is drawn with the run seed (`LevelUpOfferGenerator`, `Run.GetLevelUpOffer`), kept until taken (`Run.TakeLevelUpPackage`); `Run.HasPendingChoice` tells that one waits. It must be taken before the next fight: the screen / game loop (#77) imposes it, `Run` does not refuse the fight. |
| **Meta-progression** | Progress kept between runs. It widens options (cards in the pool, classes, bestiary, grimoire), not raw stat power. |
| **Mini-boss** | A mythical creature found in a secret room. In code: `EnemyRank.MiniBoss`. |
| **Neighbour modifier** | A bonus a card grants, each time it resolves, to the next or the previous card in its own spell line (direction set per modifier). Kinds: **+X damage**, **+X heal** or **+X shield**, X from data. It applies to one cast of that neighbour, then is used up; several bonuses on the same cast add up. It adds only to an effect of the same kind, once per cast: a +damage bonus on a card with no damage effect does nothing and is shown as **wasted** in the combat log. The line loops (the next card after the last is the first), a card alone in its line is its own neighbour, and a "previous" bonus applies on the next loop. In code: `Game.Core.Cards.NeighbourModifier`. See [ADR 0002](adr/0002-first-pass-combat-rules.md), [ADR 0004](adr/0004-confirm-first-pass-combat-rules.md) and [ADR 0005](adr/0005-neighbour-modifier-resolution.md). |
| **Objective** | A biome goal of the form "defeat N of monster X": N and the enemy come from data, and every enemy of that kind defeated in a regular fight won counts. Completing it unlocks a secret room. In code: `Game.Core.Runs.ObjectiveDefinition`; progress is in `Run.SecretRooms`. |
| **Passive upgrade** | The non-card half of a linked choice: an improvement to the character for the rest of the run. Kinds: base stats (max health, starting shield), a bonus to every effect of one kind, stronger neighbour bonuses. The same passive stacks. An effect bonus raises every effect of its kind on the hero's cards (two damage effects on a card get +2X); a neighbour bonus raises every neighbour modifier of the hero's cards, whatever its kind. Both readings were confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-04. An amount of 0 in data becomes X once upgraded. In code: `Game.Core.Upgrades.PassiveUpgrade`, applied by `PassiveUpgradeSet`, authored as `PassiveUpgradeAsset`; the MVP pool (10 passives, ids `PASSIVE_A`...`PASSIVE_J`) is the `PassiveUpgradePoolAsset` `PASSIVE_POOL_A`, whose `ToUpgrades()` is what the caller passes to `Run.GetLevelUpOffer`. See [ADR 0012](adr/0012-linked-choices-and-spell-line-editing.md). |
| **Preparation phase** | The step before a mini-boss or professor fight where the player studies the boss, rearranges the spell line and may swap cards with the reserve. It cannot be retried. See [Boss preparation phase and recap](GAME_DESIGN.md#boss-preparation-phase-and-recap). In code: `Game.Core.Runs.BossPreparation`, opened with `Run.BeginPreparation`; `Start()` ends the phase and fixes the line. The professor's data comes only from the bestiary (`ProfessorKnowledge`). The screen is `Game.Unity.UI.PreparationScreenView` over `PreparationViewModel` (#84): simple controls, no drag and drop yet; hidden professor information is shown as "Unknown". |
| **Professor** | The boss of a biome. In code: `EnemyRank.Professor`. |
| **Reserve** | The cards the player owns that are not in the spell line, for example a card gained while the line is full. No size limit. Cards are swapped between the reserve and the line during regular fights and the preparation phase. See [ADR 0009](adr/0009-vertical-slice-run-pacing.md) and [ADR 0014](adr/0014-boss-preparation-and-recap.md). |
| **Recap** | The report after a mini-boss or professor fight, or any defeat, built from the combat log: per-card output, bonuses used and wasted, and on a defeat where the chain broke (turning point and cause). See [ADR 0014](adr/0014-boss-preparation-and-recap.md). In code: `Game.Core.Combat.Recap.FightRecap`, built by `FightRecapBuilder`. |
| **Run** | One attempt, from start to defeat or victory, lasting 20 to 30 minutes across several biomes. For now a run is one biome (Vertical slice): defeating the professor wins it. Losing any fight ends the run. Between fights the player picks the next **step**: a regular fight, an unlocked secret room or the professor. In code: `Game.Core.Runs.Run`. |
| **Run screen** | The main screen of a run: the level, XP, objectives, line capacity and reserve, the choice of the next step between fights, and the fight watched tick by tick as flat shapes, with a playback speed. During regular fights the player rearranges the line and swaps with the reserve live, and waiting neighbour bonuses show on their slots. It asks the level-up choice screen to open while a level-up waits (no fight starts before), and the preparation screen before a mini-boss or the professor. See [ADR 0012](adr/0012-linked-choices-and-spell-line-editing.md). In code: `Game.Unity.UI.RunScreen.RunScreenController` (flow), `RunScreenViewModel` (what is shown), `RunScreenView` (UI Toolkit). |
| **Screen host** | The UI object that shows one screen at a time inside the single bootstrap scene, replacing the previous screen (ADR 0008). A screen can ask to be told when it stops being shown, to release what it registered. In code: `Game.Unity.UI.ScreenHost`. |
| **Secret room** | A room unlocked by completing an objective (defeat N of monster X), entered by clicking. Hides a mini-boss. The first victory over it gives a spell line slot, a unique card and revealed professor information. See [ADR 0010](adr/0010-secret-rooms-and-mini-boss-rewards.md). In code: `Game.Core.Runs.SecretRoomDefinition` (data), `SecretRoomStatus` (progress in a run), `SecretRoomRewards` (what the first victory gave, including the revelation the caller records in the bestiary with `RevealTo`). |
| **Shield** | Combat stat that absorbs damage before health: damage first reduces shield, and only the remainder reduces health. Starts at a value from data (can be zero) and can be gained during a fight. Healing does not restore it. No maximum. |
| **Spell line** | The player's build: an ordered sequence of cards that plays automatically in a loop. Position matters. Its **capacity** (number of slots) starts at 4 and grows by one the first time each mini-boss is defeated. |
| **Tick** | The discrete unit of time in a fight. The simulation advances tick by tick, never by real time. A fight that reaches the global maximum number of ticks counts as a defeat (**time limit**, [ADR 0011](adr/0011-enemy-tiers-and-fight-time-limit.md); in code: `Game.Core.Runs.RunRules.FightTimeLimit`). |
| **Turning point** | In the recap of a lost fight, the first tick from which the hero stays behind until the end: the share of health the hero has left is lower than the enemies' share (their total health over their total max health, dead enemies at zero; shield not counted, a tie is not behind). The recap looks for the causes of the defeat in the hero's last loop of casts before it, by priority: wasted bonuses, broken shield, weakest card. Confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-04. See [Boss preparation phase and recap](GAME_DESIGN.md#boss-preparation-phase-and-recap). |
| **Word** | The text of a card: 2 to 5 letters, ancient-sounding, mostly meaningless (Latin-like). Final words are written by the project owner. |
| **XP** | Experience the hero earns in a run: the XP reward of every enemy of an encounter, given when the hero wins it (regular fights, secret rooms including repeat fights, professor). A lost fight gives none. Mini-bosses simply carry more XP in their data. In code: `EnemyDefinition.XpReward`, `Run.TotalXp`. |
